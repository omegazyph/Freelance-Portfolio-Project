------------------------------------------------------------

This tool extracts the Date and Total Amount from PDF 
invoices and saves them to a CSV file for easy tracking.

🚀 HOW TO USE:
1. Place your PDF files inside the 'Invoices' folder.
2. Double-click 'run_basic_tool.bat' to start.
3. Once finished, open the 'Output' folder to find your 
   'Extracted_Data.csv' file.

🛠 REQUIREMENTS:
- Python 3.x installed.
- Required libraries: pdfplumber, pandas.
- (Run 'pip install -r requirements.txt' if needed).
