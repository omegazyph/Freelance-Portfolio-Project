Data Type	     Regex Pattern	                       Description

Standard Date	\d{1,2}[/-]\d{1,2}[/-]\d{2,4}	     Matches 12/17/1975, 12-17-75, etc.
Euro Date	    \d{1,2}\.\d{1,2}\.\d{4}	             Matches European style 17.12.1975.
Currency	   `[\d,.]+\s?(?:USD	                 EUR
Invoice No	   `(?:Inv	Invoice
Email	       [\w.-]+@[\w.-]+\.\w+	                 Useful if the client wants to extract the vendor email.
