# 📑 Invoice Data Extractor & Organizer (Standard)

Author: omegazyph | Updated: 2026-02-11

## ✨ Standard Tier Exclusive Features

* **One-Click Launch:** Includes `run_tool.bat` for easy execution.
* **Auto-File Renaming:** Automatically renames PDFs to `Date_Amount.pdf` for better archiving.
* **CSV Reporting:** Full data extraction to `Output/Report.csv`.

## 🚀 Usage

1. Place PDFs in the `Invoices/` folder.
2. Double-click `run_tool.bat`.
3. Your PDFs will be renamed and your CSV will be ready!
